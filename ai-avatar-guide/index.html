1 Record your video · HeyGen

The look of your avatar

This footage is what HeyGen learns your face and body language from. Clean, well-lit video = a realistic avatar. Shaky, dark, or busy video = a fake-looking one. Here is exactly how to shoot it.

01

How to record

  • Use a phone (newer model) or a camera on a tripod. Never handheld.
  • Shoot in 1080p or 4K, landscape (horizontal), at 30fps.
  • Frame yourself from the chest up, centered, with a little space above your head.
  • Look straight into the lens, like you're talking to one person.
  • Turn off any beauty filters, portrait mode, or auto zoom.
02

How long & what to do

  • Give us at least 4–5 minutes of usable footage per look.
  • Spend most of it talking naturally (read anything, tell a story, describe your day).
  • Include 30–60 seconds staying mostly still and quiet, looking at the camera.
  • Use your hands and normal expressions, but keep them inside the frame.
  • Keep your head and shoulders in view the whole time. Don't walk off.
03

Different looks & locations

  • Record 2–3 separate looks so we can make more than one avatar.
  • Each look = a different outfit + a different background.
  • Good backgrounds: a plain wall, a tidy office, a clean home setting.
  • Keep the background simple and uncluttered (no busy shelves, no moving people).
  • Shoot each look as its own full clip (the 4–5 min from step 02 applies to each).
04

Lighting

  • Face a window or a soft light so the light hits your face from the front.
  • Your face should be the brightest thing in the frame.
  • No window or bright light behind you (it turns you into a shadow).
  • Avoid harsh shadows across your face. Soft and even is the goal.
  • Keep the same lighting for the whole clip. Don't change it halfway.
Wardrobe

What to wear (and what to skip)

✓ Do this
  • Solid, mid-tone colours (blue, grey, muted tones)
  • Clothes that fit well and sit still on your shoulders
  • A different outfit for each look you record
  • Normal, everyday clothing you'd actually wear on camera
✕ Don't do this
  • Thin stripes, checks, or busy patterns (they flicker on video)
  • Big logos or lots of text on your clothes
  • Pure bright white or pure black (they lose detail)
  • Shiny jewellery, lanyards, or anything that dangles and moves
  • Bright green clothing (it can disappear on some backgrounds)
05

The consent clip (required)

HeyGen requires a short clip of you giving permission to build your avatar. It takes 20 seconds. Look at the camera and say the line below clearly.

Read this on camera

"I, [your full name], give my consent to create and use an AI avatar and AI voice of myself with HeyGen and ElevenLabs."

We'll send you the exact official wording before you record, but this is essentially it.

06

How to export & send

  • Export at the original, highest quality (do not shrink or compress).
  • File type: .MP4 or .MOV.
  • No music, no captions, no on-screen text baked into the video.
  • Don't send via WhatsApp (it crushes the quality). Upload the raw file to the Drive folder.
  • Name each file clearly (see the folder guide below).
2 Record your voice · ElevenLabs

The sound of your avatar

We clone your voice in ElevenLabs from clean audio recordings. The cleaner and longer the audio, the more it actually sounds like you, not a robot. Here's how to capture it.

01

How much audio

  • Bare minimum: 30 minutes of clean audio.
  • Sweet spot: 60–90 minutes. This is where it starts sounding truly like you.
  • More is fine, as long as it stays clean and consistent.
  • It can be several recordings added together, not one long take.
02

How it should sound

  • Only your voice. No other people talking in the background.
  • No background noise — no fan, TV, traffic, music, or echo.
  • Record in a quiet room with soft things (sofa, curtains, carpet) to kill echo.
  • Keep the mic the same distance from your mouth the whole time.
  • Speak in your normal, natural tone — the way you'd talk in a video.
03

What to record with

  • A decent USB mic or a good phone held close is enough. No studio needed.
  • Wired earphones with a mic work well if held steady.
  • Export as WAV if possible (best), or high-quality M4A/MP3.
  • Good news: the clean audio from your video shoot counts too — send that as well.
04

What to actually say

  • Just talk naturally for the recording — this matters more than the words.
  • Read a few articles or scripts out loud (mix calm and energetic bits).
  • Then speak off the cuff: explain your work, answer common client questions.
  • Include a few numbers, dates, and your key industry words so the voice learns them.
Example — read this to warm up

"Hi, my name is [name]. Today is a normal working day and I want to walk you through how I think about this. There's no rush here. I'll speak clearly, keep my pace steady, and cover a few real examples so you get the full picture."

Read that at your natural pace, then keep going into your own topic. Thirty to forty minutes of relaxed talking like this, plus the audio from your video shoot, usually gets us to the 60–90 minute sweet spot.

Quick answers

Common questions

Do I need a professional camera?
No. A recent phone on a tripod, with good lighting, gives us exactly what we need. The lighting and the steady tripod matter far more than the camera itself.
Do I have to memorise a script?
Not at all. For both the video and the voice, you just talk naturally. Read an article, tell a story, explain your work. The AI learns your face and voice, not your words.
Can I record it in parts over a few days?
Yes. Just try to keep the lighting and audio setup similar each time so it stays consistent. Then drop every file into the same shared folder.
What if I mispronounce or fumble a word?
No problem. Just pause, and say the line again. We cut the mistakes. Natural pauses and re-takes are completely fine.
How long does this take me?
Most people finish all the recording in one afternoon. And you only ever do this once — after that, we generate unlimited videos from your avatar.
Is my footage and voice kept private?
Yes. Your files are only used to build your avatar and voice. We don't share them, and the consent clip is required by the platforms specifically to protect you.
