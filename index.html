You've Got Questions,
We've Got Answers.

Who is Ripple NOT for? +
Ripple is not for founders who post sporadically. If you're not creating video content at least 3–4 times a week, distribution won't have enough material to work with. We also don't work with illegal, harmful, or NSFW content.
What results can I expect? +
Most clients see a 5×–20× increase in views per video within the first 30 days, thousands of new followers monthly, and inbound leads from audiences who've never heard of them before — all from content they've already created.
How is Ripple different from hiring clip editors? +
Clipping is an uncontrolled market — 20% of editors capture 80% of results, while the rest produce inconsistent output. But the deeper problem is control: a clip editor builds their own page, and the moment they delete it, rename it, or pivot their content — your audience disappears overnight.

With Ripple, you own 100% of your content. Our distribution infrastructure is built around your brand, not someone else's. And because we've engineered the process, we deliver comparable (often better) results at 10× lower cost than running a full clipping operation.
Why can't I just grow my own account organically? +
You absolutely should — and we help you do that faster. But algorithms alone are unpredictable. Ripple adds a distribution layer on top of your organic effort, so one piece of content reaches audiences that the algorithm would never show it to. You stop depending on luck.
Do I need a big audience to begin? +
Not at all. Most of our strongest results come from founders starting near zero. What matters is that you create quality content with a clear message. Ripple handles getting that message in front of millions of new eyes.
Won't clip editors' audiences just drift away over time? +
Exactly — and this is a critical insight. Clip editors only ever create derivative content. The moment a creator stops producing, or the editor moves on, the content dies. There's no long-term asset being built.

Ripple distributes your original content — videos you own, messages you control. Every view compounds back into your brand, not a third-party page that could vanish tomorrow.
How much time does it take from me? +
Almost none. You keep creating — we handle everything else. Most clients spend fewer than 30 minutes per week on anything Ripple-related. No managing editors, no chasing deadlines, no worrying about page quality.
What platforms do you work with? +
We distribute primarily on Instagram, TikTok, and YouTube Shorts — the three highest-growth platforms for short-form content in 2026. We can expand to Facebook and X depending on your audience profile.
Do you have a guarantee? +
Yes. If you don't see a measurable increase in reach within your first 60 days, we work for free until you do. We only win when you win.
How much does it cost? +
Pricing depends on the scale of distribution you need. Book a call — spots are limited intentionally so we can maintain quality for every founder we work with.
